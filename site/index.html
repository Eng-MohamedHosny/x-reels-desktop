Install in a minute

Three steps. That’s it.

Download and unzip

Get x-reels-desktop-1.0.0.zip and unzip it into a folder you’ll keep.

Load it in your browser

Open chrome://extensions, edge://extensions or brave://extensions, turn on Developer mode, then click Load unpacked.

Press the blue button

Open x.com, click the X Reels icon and press the button. Open a video and start scrolling.
